🌲 Blockside
Adventure

A tiny block-world adventure. Walk around as a blocky explorer, look around by dragging the right side of the screen, cut blocks with the CUT button, then build with them.

Mobile: hold the joystick to walk · drag the right side to look · CUT to break the block in the crosshair · BUILD to place one.

Computer: WASD to move · mouse to look · Space to jump · left-click to cut · right-click to build.

Three.js voxel sandbox · best in landscape mode on phones
